Our Story

We started Ichiro because most consulting felt like theatre.

Too many engagements end in a 200-slide strategy deck and no working software. Ichiro Consulting exists to do the opposite: small senior teams, direct communication, and systems that are still running — and still loved by the people who use them — years later.

We work with founders who need to move fast and enterprises that need to move safely, often at the same time. Whatever the constraint, our job is to make the software disappear into the background so your business can get on with business.

James

Founder & Principal Consultant

James founded Ichiro Consulting after a decade building and scaling engineering teams across fintech, logistics, and retail. Tired of watching good engineering get buried under bad process, he set out to build a consultancy that ships first and presents second.

These days he splits his time between client architecture reviews, hiring the next generation of Ichiro engineers, and personally reading every single project retro — because the details are where projects actually succeed or fail.
