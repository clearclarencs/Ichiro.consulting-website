Our Story

We started Ichiro because most consulting felt like theatre.

Too many engagements end in a bloated strategy deck and no working software. Ichiro Consulting exists to do the opposite: direct communication, hands-on senior engineering, and systems built to last.

We work with founders who need to move fast and businesses that need to move safely — often at the same time. Whatever the constraint, our job is to make the software disappear into the background so you can get on with running your business.

James

Founder & Principal Consultant

James founded Ichiro Consulting after a career spent building and scaling software systems. Tired of watching good engineering get buried under bad process, he set out to build a consultancy that ships first and presents second.

He leads every engagement personally — from the first architecture conversation through to delivery — because the details are where projects actually succeed or fail.
