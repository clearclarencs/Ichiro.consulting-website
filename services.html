Our Services

Everything you need to design, build, and run modern software.

Pick a single engagement or bring us in end-to-end. Either way, you get senior engineers, clear communication, and software that's built to last.

Cloud Architecture & Migration

We move you off fragile, expensive, or unsupported infrastructure and onto cloud-native architecture that scales with demand instead of fighting it — with a migration plan built around zero downtime.

  • AWS, Azure, and GCP architecture design
  • Lift-and-shift, re-platform, and full re-architecture paths
  • Cost optimization audits to cut unnecessary cloud spend
  • Multi-region failover and disaster recovery planning

AI & Machine Learning Integration

We build practical machine learning into products and operations — forecasting, recommendation engines, document intelligence, and automation — grounded in your actual data, not hype.

  • LLM-powered internal tools and customer-facing assistants
  • Predictive analytics and demand forecasting models
  • Document processing and intelligent automation pipelines
  • Responsible AI review: bias, evaluation, and guardrails

Custom Software Development

Web apps, mobile apps, and internal tooling built around how your team actually works — not forced into an off-the-shelf template that fights you at every turn.

  • Full-stack web and mobile application development
  • Internal tools, dashboards, and admin platforms
  • API design and third-party integrations
  • Design systems and component libraries your team can extend

DevOps & Infrastructure Automation

We build the pipelines, monitoring, and infrastructure-as-code that let your team ship confidently and sleep through the night.

  • CI/CD pipeline design and automation
  • Infrastructure as code (Terraform, Pulumi, CloudFormation)
  • Observability: logging, metrics, tracing, and alerting
  • Incident response runbooks and on-call readiness

Legacy System Modernization

We untangle decades-old systems and re-platform them incrementally, so you get modern architecture without a risky big-bang rewrite.

  • Monolith-to-services decomposition
  • Database modernization and zero-downtime migrations
  • Strangler-fig rollouts alongside existing systems
  • Technical debt audits with a prioritized remediation roadmap

Cybersecurity & Compliance Consulting

Security and compliance built into the architecture from day one — so audits become a formality instead of a fire drill.

  • SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR readiness assessments

  • Security-first architecture reviews for new products
